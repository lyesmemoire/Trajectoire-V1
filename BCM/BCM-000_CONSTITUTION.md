## Preamble

This Constitution establishes the Blueprint Cognitive Model (BCM) as the universal computational theory of cognition for Blueprint Enterprise. The BCM is the physics of intelligence, independent of any domain, language model, cloud provider, or business application.

**Principle**: The BCM describes only how intelligence works, not what it does.

---

## Philosophy

### Core Philosophy

**The Blueprint Cognitive Model is founded on these immutable principles:**

1. **Universality**: The BCM applies to all cognitive systems, regardless of domain or implementation.
2. **Cognitive Determinism**: Cognitive processes are deterministic given the same inputs and internal state.
3. **Complete Traceability**: Every cognitive operation must be fully traceable from stimulus to decision.
4. **Verifiability**: All cognitive processes must be mathematically verifiable.
5. **Explainability**: Every cognitive decision must be explainable in human-understandable terms.
6. **Composition**: Cognitive processes must be composable from primitive operations.
7. **Extensibility**: The BCM must be extensible without breaking existing components.
8. **Modularity**: Cognitive processes must be modular and independently testable.
9. **Executability**: The BCM must be executable on the Blueprint runtime.
10. **Compatibility**: The BCM must be compatible with Blueprint DSL, Semantic Compiler, COS, CVM, CPR, and CCP.

### Independence Principles

**The BCM is independent of:**

- Language models (LLMs)
- Prompts
- OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, or any AI provider
- Cloud providers
- Business domains (recruitment, sales, finance, legal, medical, etc.)
- Applications
- Databases
- Runtime implementations

**The BCM describes only cognitive abstractions, formal contracts, and executable models.**

2. **Consistency**: Cognitive processes maintain internal consistency across time.
3. **Replayability**: Cognitive processes can be replayed exactly.
4. **Traceability**: Every cognitive operation can be traced from stimulus to response.
5. **Explainability**: Every cognitive decision can be explained in human-understandable terms.
6. **Safety**: Cognitive processes operate within defined safety boundaries.
7. **Isolation**: Cognitive processes are isolated from external interference.
8. **Auditability**: All cognitive operations are auditable.
9. **Composability**: Cognitive processes can be composed from primitives.
10. **Extensibility**: The BCM can be extended without breaking existing components.

**Observation**: Raw data captured from the environment
**Perception**: Interpreted observation with context
**Attention**: Selection of relevant information
**Context**: Current situation and history
**Evidence**: Information supporting beliefs
**Knowledge**: Structured information about the world
**Belief**: Information held as true with confidence
**Hypothesis**: Tentative explanation or prediction
**Reasoning**: Process of drawing conclusions from premises
**Inference**: Conclusion drawn from reasoning
**Decision**: Choice among alternatives
**Plan**: Sequence of actions to achieve a goal
**Execution**: Carrying out a plan or decision
**Learning**: Process of acquiring knowledge
**Memory**: Storage and retrieval of information
**Adaptation**: Process of adjusting to changing conditions
**Meta-Reasoning**: Reasoning about reasoning
**Self-Evaluation**: Evaluation of own performance

**Confidence Vector**: Multi-dimensional representation of confidence

**Cognitive State**: Internal state of cognitive processes
